Fruit Waste Nutritional Guide

Understanding the core biochemical compounds present in common organic fruit waste.

Watermelon Peel

Key Nutrients: High Water Content, Organic Fiber, Citrulline, Micro-elements.
Soil Benefits: Retains moisture, prevents root drying, and fuels microflora proliferation in the rhizosphere.
Ideal for Hydration & Microbes

Melon Peel

Key Nutrients: Abundant in Potassium (K), Phosphorus (P), and Essential Minerals.
Soil Benefits: Stimulates flowering, enhances crop flavor/sweetness, and improves overall disease tolerance.
Ideal for Flowering & Fruiting

Papaya Peel

Key Nutrients: Papain Enzymes, Nitrogen (N) Compounds, Vitamins A & C.
Soil Benefits: Accelerates vegetative tissue growth, triggers rapid root branching, and breaks down organic matter.
Ideal for Foliage & Root Expansion

LOF & Compost Guide

Detailed instructions on manufacturing and applying Fruitera Organic Products.

1. Making Process (LOF & Solid Compost)

Step-by-step anaerobic extraction from raw fruit waste.

6 Steps
1

Collection & Sorting

Gather fresh peels of Watermelon, Melon, and Papaya. Thoroughly separate non-organic items (plastics, stickers, synthetic ties) to avoid chemical contamination.

2

Washing & Fine Chopping

Rinse fruit peels to remove dirt. Slice them finely into 1-2 cm pieces. Smaller surface areas significantly accelerate microbial decomposition.

3

Bio-Activator Preparation

Mix 1 Liter of clean water with 20ml EM4 (Effective Microorganisms) and 20ml brown sugar solution / molasses as bacterial food to trigger anaerobic fermentation.

4

Airtight Fermentation

Seal chopped peels and bio-activator in an airtight container for 14-21 days. Briefly open the lid every 2 days to release accumulated bio-gas.

5

Liquid Extraction (LOF)

Filter the fermented liquid through a fine mesh cloth or sieve. The harvested clear liquid is your highly concentrated Fruitera Liquid Organic Fertilizer.

6

Solid Composting

Do not discard the solid pulp residue! Mix remaining organic solid residue with dry leaves and garden soil (1:2 ratio) to create nutrient-rich Fruitera Solid Compost.

2. Application Method & Dosage Guide

Dilution standards, target crops, and optimal scheduling.

Golden Dilution Ratio: 1 : 10

Mix 1 part Fruitera LOF with 10 parts clean water (e.g., 100 ml LOF to 1 Liter water). Never apply undiluted concentrated LOF directly onto roots or foliage.

Direct Soil Drenching

Pour diluted solution near the root zone on moist soil. Ideal for nutrient absorption by primary roots.

Foliar Spraying

Spray fine mist on undersides of leaves where stomata are active. Avoid spraying in peak sunlight hours.

Crop Specific Dosage Guidelines:

Plant Category Examples Diluted Dosage per Plant Frequency
Ornamental Plants Monstera, Roses, Ferns 100 - 150 ml Every 1 - 2 weeks
Leafy Vegetables Lettuce, Spinach, Kale 150 - 200 ml Every 7 - 10 days
Fruiting Crops Chili, Tomato, Cucumber 250 - 300 ml Every 5 - 7 days (Flowering)
Recommended Application Hours:
Early morning (6:00 AM - 9:00 AM) or late afternoon (4:00 PM - 6:00 PM) to avoid leaf burn and rapid UV evaporation.
Pro Tip:
Always shake bottled liquid fertilizer thoroughly before measuring to re-suspend settled organic micro-solids.
